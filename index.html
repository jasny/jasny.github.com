Hi, I'm Arnold Daniels. I've been building software since 2000, currently as a senior engineer at Navara. I'm mostly interested in how systems are structured, how abstractions hold up over time, and where complexity actually belongs.

My focus areas are architecture, distributed systems, workflow engines, and developer tooling. I care about building things that remain coherent as they grow, systems where the structure reflects the problem, not just the deadline.

Letsrunit

Letsrunit is an AI-driven end-to-end testing platform for agentic workflows. It shows what breaks and why, making AI-generated functionality reproducible and controllable within CI-driven environments.

Arnold is the architect and creator of this project, combining AI with test-driven development for modern agentic systems.

  • TypeScript
  • AI agents
  • E2E testing
  • CI/CD

LetsFlow

LetsFlow is a human-centric workflow automation engine that blends automation with human decision-making. It models processes as finite-state machines where actors — people, organizations, or systems — perform actions to drive state transitions.

Arnold is the architect and creator of this project, building on his experience designing workflow engines for legal and compliance use cases.

  • TypeScript
  • Node.js
  • Workflow engine
  • Event sourcing

EQTY Ownables

Ownables are a new kind of digital asset based on blockchain technology — private, secure, and always in your control. They can represent anything from digital collectibles to real-world assets, enabling the next generation of Web3 applications.

Arnold is the architect of this project, designed within the EQTY ecosystem.
